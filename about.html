About

Researcher, engineer and teacher working on AI for medical images.

My PhD at NIT Durgapur designed lightweight segmentation networks and an end-to-end system for liver tumour segmentation and treatment response assessment from CT images. The work introduced new feature refinement modules, recurrent attention mechanisms and adaptive activation functions, and produced SCI journal papers, conference papers and two published Indian patents.

Today I build AI for dental imaging in industry. Through ByteDecode I publish independent research and teach, in plain Hinglish on YouTube.
